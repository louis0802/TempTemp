# Semantic contract v2 — frozen before extraction

Primary classification concerns the explicit primary economic proposition. Definite promotion requires its exact identifying quote; ordinary product prices are not inherently promotions. Explicit sale/deal cards can identify an economic campaign while benefit remains null if no quantified incentive exists. Mixed editorial/secondary contest or missing-media ambiguity stays uncertain. An economic incentive clearly separable from an article may be extracted; incidental contests cannot replace the article's proposition.

Schedule and positive location facts require both literal evidence and an exact association quote. These may be the same span if it relates the fact to the offer. An app release/check day, contest submission period, voucher issuance deadline or publication date is not primary validity. Missing/ambiguous association means null. Explicit source years only. No single global hours/weekday flattening across differently timed groups.

Scope is positive all_outlets, selected_outlets, named_outlets or null. Selected unnamed participation stays selected_outlets. Explicit named participants use named_outlets, including a selected list whose names are stated. Scope needs actual positive coverage wording; outlet types/merchant-only/channel references do not declare coverage. Exclusion-only statements leave scope null. locationRules preserve source labels with participating/excluded polarity. No inferred positive membership from absence in an exclusion list. Full supported labels and reviewed short identities describe the same outlet and are evaluated separately from canonical normalization.

| Role | Frozen meaning | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| eligibility | Who qualifies: status, age, membership, valid ID, designated beneficiary | Lowest-paying free diner is eligibility. Required paying party size is purchase_requirement. |
| purchase_requirement | Purchase prerequisite, minimum spend/quantity, required full-paying diners | Selected/same-price product limitations are item_restriction. |
| redemption_channel | Redemption method/channel, app/code, dine-in/takeaway/pickup, cashier, reservation | Explicitly barred channels are exclusion. Scope is separate. Spoken redemption phrase/knowledge challenge is a redemption method. |
| social_requirement | Required follow/post/tag/public-account action | An account handle alone proves neither merchant identity nor outlet participation. |
| timing_requirement | Applicable validity/order/seating/time window, dining duration, redemption frequency, advance notice | Holiday blackouts are exclusion. Secondary deadlines are omitted. Recommendations may remain recommendations verbatim. |
| item_restriction | Qualifying/selected/same-price products, item availability, quantity cap, modifiers/top-ups | Required item purchase is purchase_requirement. One-use-per-order redemption frequency is timing_requirement. |
| exclusion | Explicit barred dates, channels, combinations, persons, locations | Positive eligibility is eligibility; exclusions alone prove no positive scope. |
| general_term | Residual administrative/disclaimer/amendment/nonexchangeability/bill calculation conditions | Never use this to erase an explicitly identifiable atomic role. |

Atomic separable clauses are split without paraphrasing. For an inseparable mixed clause, its controlling requirement determines the role. Combined source statements can contain several independently supported roles; grouping differences are reviewed, not automatically called invented facts. Do not duplicate a clause across roles. Restriction omissions are coverage losses, distinct from role errors and invention.

Benefit is a verbatim incentive clause (value equals quote), retaining material qualifiers. An alternative correctly described incentive in the same primary offer is valid; numerical starting/up-to qualifications must survive in the representation. Minimum purchases can be retained as explicit purchase_requirement items. Generic sale wording does not prove a numeric discount. Merchant must appear in source and extracted merchant quote; context/registry identity remains separate. Title is source-supported and cannot switch proposition.

The validator checks strict enums, exact quotes/associations, source label/text containment, dates/years/ranges, weekdays/hour forms, null coherence and contradictory declared roles. It cannot determine whether an exact quote semantically supports its claimed role. In particular, a positive label placed on a negative sentence can pass syntactic checks; benchmark tests and source review must catch it. There is no exclusion-keyword or merchant semantic parser in the validator. A2 requires source-reviewed safety, not merely quote acceptance.

Forty-nine reviewed annotations are parent source review, not independent human gold. The parent necessarily saw sources/v1 gold while authoring these; fresh evaluation agents see only the allowlisted source/hints/prompt/schema. Preparation freezes hashes; evaluation gold loading/scoring occurs only after all raw results are sealed. No production mapping is implemented.

Evaluation counts raw and accepted semantic errors separately. Automated annotation comparisons are proposals: every response is source-reviewed, including unflagged facts and unmatched restriction clauses. Parent review records explicit confirmed/dismissed reasons without editing frozen gold or repairing outputs. A valid source span with the wrong role/association is an error; label normalization, source-backed wording or harmless overlapping clause grouping is not invention. Omitted constraints are reported separately. Malformed output is unassessable, not proof of safety.

Per-case comparison rubric: malformed v2 takes precedence (note if v1 also malformed), otherwise malformed v1; among usable pairs, any new high-risk error makes regressed; fewer high-risk errors, corrected primary classification or recovered material constraints makes improved; pure role/label regrouping without semantic change is schema-role difference only; remaining equivalent behavior is unchanged. Mixed gains/losses must be disclosed. Restriction-only improvements/regressions are separately noted and cannot cancel a new high-risk error. V1 overloaded fields have no equivalent eight-role score; do not invent a v1 role-error count.

For a useful classification signal in this small fixed corpus, report both versions' precision/recall/non-promotion correctness and uncertainty plus actual error cases. A2 needs the stated zero high-risk source-reviewed gate and conservative uncertain/null treatment of ambiguous propositions; a positive score trend cannot override a single invented validity/participation/contamination/year error. This research can recommend at most A2 and never authorize an API run, adapter changes or production activation.
