## FairPrice

Start was the public [promotions listing](https://www.fairprice.com.sg/promotions), not an individual product. Its directly delivered __NEXT_DATA__ reports page 1, 20 products and 50 total pages. Product offers include explicit promotion IDs, buy/variant associations, DELIVERY order type and validFrom/validTill timestamps. Those timestamps have unproven campaign/time-zone semantics and are not used as campaign expiry. No undocumented API or application JS was inspected. Visible pricing, member pricing and product stock do not prove physical participation.

The visibly linked [weekly campaign root](https://www.fairprice.com.sg/weekly-promotions) supplies public inline publication objects: 24 exact IDs/URLs/title/description/pageCount records, pagination page 1/pageSize 45/totalPages 1. Exact DOM cards repeat the title and campaign period. Two linked promotions.fairprice.com.sg catalogue pages return 200 HTML viewer shells with no accessible body text. Their pageCount is brochure length, not a count of independently enumerable offers/products. The adapter preserves catalogue-level metadata only; one UNITY catalogue retains unknown promoted merchant rather than becoming FairPrice by default. Benefits/product sets/participation remain reviewable gaps. Listing completeness for these 24 catalogues does not establish completeness of all FairPrice campaigns.

Independent operator evidence is the [official service terms](https://help.fairprice.com.sg/hc/en-us/articles/360025882372-Terms-Conditions), which explicitly identifies the organisation providing fairprice.com.sg. This declaration was readable through public research; ordinary direct GET received 403 and is retained without bypass. Existing frozen ownership was already verified. A guessed /events/weekly-promotions/ path returned 404 and is never registered. Acquisition roots contain only the demonstrated weekly root. No store directory is fetched because no campaign proves an all-store scope.

## Kris+

The [exact website conditions](https://www.krisplus.com/en/sg/terms-and-conditions) independently declare that this website is owned by Kris+ Pte Ltd. This is legal operator evidence, rather than branding. Directory navigation connects to Singapore Airlines [operator terms](https://www.singaporeair.com/en_UK/sg/ppsclub-krisflyer/use-miles/krisplus/terms-of-use/). Some Singapore Airlines research URLs were readable via search but returned direct 404; only the directly captured exact-site declaration is needed for this ownership conclusion.

Fresh HTML contains twelve cards. Inline rendered Next page state reports totalCount=29/currentPage=1/totalPages=3 and an enabled Load more button. There are no linked page URLs. No documented/request-visible backend was established with bounded HTTP alone. Reading minified bundles to guess an endpoint would not prove the allowed public-page boundary, so no such calls were made. Source remains partial/shadow and default limits suffice for the visible subset.

All twelve card URLs were captured. A single PromotionDetail article and exact title/subtitle form the campaign boundary. The Birthday Bash page now explicitly describes a multi-partner 8mpd campaign; this does not prove correspondence to the historical app alias. It stays one object with merchant unknown and its explicit participant scope preserved. Esso and iStudio have clear public merchant declarations; the Zouk-group article contains seven named merchants and multiple embedded offer scopes, so it remains one object with merchant unknown. Generic “View Partner” buttons are navigation, not merchant names. Other ambiguous/platform-wide identities remain unknown. An Esso Mastercard suboffer has its own dates: these must not become the expiry of the header's ongoing fuel offer. App redirect anchors can supply their public labels, but are never requested and cannot fill app-only facts. Generic publication currently has one configured merchant/category per source; partner campaigns therefore remain shadow without changing that architecture.

## Dian Xiao Er

The public promo, home, membership and [outlet directory](https://www.dianxiaoer.com.sg/outlets) were inspected separately. Contact/copyright branding and membership links do not independently establish the legal operator, so ownership remains probable. No production activation relies on it.

The collection is #comp-m593fqa4, data-testid=slide-show-gallery. Three ghost cards represent 20% Early Bird, 30% Early Bird and Citibank 1 for 1 Soup; one current slide duplicates the first. Each has its own gallery-item-title, gallery-item-description, aria-describedby identity and imageData URI/dimensions. Counter 1/7 proves partial rendering. A known current/ghost mirror may deduplicate only when identity and own text agree; duplicate/conflicting ghosts fail closed.

Inline wix-viewer-model/warmup data identify the slideshow component but do not supply a complete seven-item campaign collection or historical dataItem-msxym06a mapping. Direct GET of the exact lightbox query returns the same card subset. No guessed Wix data endpoints or browser automation are justified for this shadow slice. The adapter uses image URI plus gallery ID as native card identity; it never fabricates a lightbox ID. Metadata is retained as evidence notes without downloading pixels. Offer benefit/dates/terms remain unavailable. Only the 30% card's own selected-outlet caption supplies names; it cannot leak into 20% Early Bird or Citibank. Directory existence does not fill missing campaign wording, and no new outlet provider is needed while ingestion is disabled.

## CS Foods

The independently linked [corporate site](https://wholesale-meats.csfoods.sg/) names Chee Song Foods Pte Ltd and explicitly links its Consumer Website to csfoods.sg. This operator/navigation evidence establishes ownership separately from acquisition. Consumer terms are generic/inconsistent in places and are not relied on to infer campaign facts or operator identity.

The sale root returns ordinary HTML with WooCommerce products and sale/bundle wording. One catalogue-linked McCain product returns a bounded 7822-byte HTTP 403 CleanTalk anti-crawler page. Product acquisition stopped immediately after this result; the catalogue and independently captured corporate/terms pages are diagnostic evidence only. No alternative hostname, human impersonation, CAPTCHA solving, stealth, retry loop or private API was attempted.

No dated campaign terms were established. Catalogue-wide descriptive sale language, product shelf-life and old/new price are different from campaign validity. Complete product pagination is neither demonstrated nor claimed, and would not establish campaign completeness. Final blocked decision has no runtime ID, factory or placeholder adapter. The research-only merchant map records adapter=none plus explicit access/validity/enumeration blockers.
