# Proposition anchor contract
Node {id, anchorEvidenceIds, propositionTypeHint}. Frozen hints: offer, contest, editorial, product, service, event, other. IDs must be unique and anchors must reference existing evidence. Supporting bundles, synthetic proposition text, validity, constraints, locations and primary selection are forbidden.
Anchor discovery sees only sealed evidence nodes in source order, hints and case ID. It sees all potential anchors so competing economic offers remain separate. Several economic/identity nodes can establish one proposition; one supporting condition does not by itself require another proposition. A meal and a chance-to-win contest, or editorial collaboration and transactional fries benefit, coexist independently.
Identity scoring maps anchors to reviewed source-backed identities and explicit equivalent clauses; false nodes, ambiguous merges and duplicated/fragmented economics are reported separately. Hint labels do not decide economic eligibility. All source-level economic identities remain in end-to-end denominator even when an upstream stage is malformed.
