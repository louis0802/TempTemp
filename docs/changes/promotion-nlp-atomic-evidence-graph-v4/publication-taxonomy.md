# Publication taxonomy
Frozen eight types: economic_offer, contest_or_chance, editorial, product_launch, store_announcement, service_information, event_or_activity, uncertain.
economic_offer requires explicit transactional/economic incentive: discount, reduced/bundle price, buy-X-get-Y, guaranteed free item conditional on purchase, meaningful loyalty benefit, or explicit deal wording with unquantified entitlement. A regular menu price or exclusive product alone does not establish an incentive.
Chance-to-win, draw, contest prize and giveaway chance are contest_or_chance. Guaranteed free item with purchase is economic_offer; the distinction is entitlement, not matching the word contest. Narrative collaboration is editorial; a product introduction product_launch; opening without incentive store_announcement; delivery/service information service_information; activity without economic entitlement event_or_activity. Missing media or ambiguous entitlement is uncertain.
Stage4 receives only one anchor and its linked evidence/relations. It never sees gold, raw full source, sibling facts, parser/prior answers or future normalizations. A merged anchor is scored as an upstream grouping error as well as a classification uncertainty, never silently accepted as one correctly classified offer. Only economic_offer continues to Stage5.
