# Atomic evidence contract
Frozen kinds: economic_claim, date, weekday, time, location, participation, channel, audience, purchase_condition, redemption_instruction, social_condition, availability, exclusion, contest, editorial, product, service, other. Kinds describe claims and never establish publication eligibility.
Node {id, quote, kind}. quote is an exact contiguous substring of whitespace-normalized isolated SOURCE_TEXT. IDs and identical quote strings are unique. No synthetic concatenation, paraphrase, summaries or corrected grammar. Overlap is allowed for association-bearing qualifications, but independent material claims should be separately identifiable. Missing filler is harmless; missing entitlement/eligibility/redemption/quantity/timing/participation/exclusion is material loss.
Equivalent boundaries are explicitly annotated exact-source alternatives. A larger node covering one semantic clause is accepted as coverage, but a span merging distinct conflicting gold claims is counted independently as harmful under-splitting. A fragment must preserve the semantic clause, not merely share a word. Structural validation does not certify atomicity or semantic recall.
Stage1 may emit Flash this page, economic discount and Takeaway only as separate nodes. Opening to 5PM is literal evidence. No proposition, classification or normalized fact fields are accepted.
