# Failure analysis

The primary research question has a negative result: sealed proposition-local input prevents direct access to omitted siblings, but Stage 1 still groups incompatible offer variants and loses associated context. Bari Salad Bar is the surviving demonstration: all-day and 2–5PM physical groups enter one unit; Stage 2 flattens its timing across all four outlets. The frozen scorer misses this because central benefit anchors do not match either group anchor; mandatory source review detects it. No scorer or gold was edited to improve the run.

Student before/after separation blocks the V2 dine-in transfer mechanically, but harmful fragmentation removes the before-offer economic context, and the after-offer output is malformed. This is not successful economic recovery. Captain Early Bird remains one risky group with three price/timing variants and is schema malformed. Local Faves retains operational Monday context, correctly leaves availability weekdays unknown, but loses mandatory login/QR/cashier clauses into unassigned text.

Local classification can still fail even with perfect separation: bfmcsaver's judged contest is called promotion. Its submission dates stay out of the meal, but become false economic-promotion validity in the new contest candidate. Collab quiz redemption and lowest-paying diner eligibility are misattributed to timing/purchase. Composable attributes reduce false exclusive choices but do not ensure meaning. Starting prices and a bill-calculation condition become purchase prerequisites.

Simpler contracts reduce observed per-output malformed rate, but eight syntax and two schema failures remain. The two-stage pipeline leaves 12/49 sources with at least one segmentation/extraction failure (two exact-span rejects plus ten extraction-failure sources); no failure is repaired. Exact copying is also imperfect: two Stage-1 outputs fail case/wording, three usable-unit evidence mismatches and three atomic text-containment failures appear.

Generic collective place labels without an all/selected prefix still pass the thin physical-name filter. These are two domain errors, not invented physical branches. Frozen constraint annotations inherited whole-clause targets with heuristic attribute conversion; atomic subsets often get unfair flags. Preserve automated totals and report every source-review disposition instead of presenting regex aliases as semantic proof.

Next work should preserve qualifier value semantics, separate differing location/time groups or retain explicit group correlation within a sealed unit, attach mandatory neutral redemption context and strengthen generic physical-domain handling. Review benchmark alternative anchors and atomic allowed-attribute sets before another blind run. No merchant-specific production patch, repair, retry or rollout was used.
