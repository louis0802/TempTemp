# Reviewed captured benchmark

Gold: `tests/fixtures/promotion-nlp/benchmark.json`. Checked provider replay: `provider-outputs.json` in the same directory. The 49 cases contain 39 promotions, 8 non-promotions and 2 uncertain cases. They were reviewed by Codex against each normalized captured block, with explicit supported-value/quote and unknown-field decisions. This is source review, not independent human annotation. Current parser output is a separately stored comparison, never automatic gold.

Coverage: Pepper Lunch 7; Shake Shack 22; Captain Kim 5; Bari Bari Steak 5; McDonald's 2; Sushiro 1; FairPrice 3; Instagram captions 4 (SHINRAI, Ajumma's Korean Restaurant, Sinpopo Brand, Starbucks Singapore). Duplicate Captain Kim text from alternate venue URLs was excluded rather than inflating the denominator.

Layouts include campaign detail with separate terms, Elementor blog body and title, Captain Kim venue sections and a takeaway heading, exact Bari listing cards, McDonald's indivisible multi-offer pages with embedded contest, an image-led Sushiro product page, individual FairPrice catalogue cards, and Instagram og:title caption suffixes. Each case records capture path/hash, URL, original evidence reference, exact selector/recipe, normalized text, classification, supported/unknown fields, scalar alternatives, rationale and parser comparison where available.

Positive facts include percentage discounts, one-for-one, free/complimentary items, bundles, explicit year-bearing/same-month date ranges, weekdays, single hour ranges, dine-in/takeaway, student/age/membership requirements, all/named/selected outlet wording and terms. Negative/ambiguous coverage includes ordinary articles, product launches, opening without economic benefit, media-dependent details, missing validity/year, publication/URL metadata exclusion, secondary contest dates, multiple propositions, unresolved participation, holiday exceptions, and hours with multiple groups or unstated opening time.

Bari only supplies exact card heading/body, with neighboring and related-post discounts excluded. The captured 1-for-1 detail template actually contains unrelated discounts, but is not input. Pepper/FairPrice siblings are likewise excluded. McDonald's meal gold deliberately excludes its 1–15 October 2026 contest and notification dates. Sushiro has no stated year and no explicit discount/free benefit, so gold classifies the captured product-launch text as non-promotion; its validity remains unknown. These labels are documented product judgments, not inherited parser labels.

`loadReviewedBenchmark` verifies capture SHA-256, rebuilds exact text with local-only recipes, checks schema and gold validity before any provider call. Construction/read performs no merchant/network acquisition. Instagram captions are conditional trusted-text examples from existing captures; they do not establish acquisition readiness or enable merchants. Images, video, OCR and private embedded payloads were not interpreted.

Scoring uses exact values/list sets and explicitly reviewed scalar alternatives. Unknowns are exhaustively partitioned. Per-field raw unsupported/caught/survived counts are gold-relative field counts; a wrong known value is also a conservative unsupported-value signal. Formatting, paraphrase and grouping differences may require review before claiming hallucination. Results preserve raw, validated and current-parser/gold values to make that review possible. Fixture outputs intentionally match gold to test the contract; replay accuracy must never be presented as LLM reliability.

Limitations: finite captured sample, mostly English, uneven merchant counts, no fresh acquisition or human inter-annotator agreement, sparse image-led details, no useful physical-outlet completeness claim, and historical/snapshot parser comparisons. There is no evidence yet for live model accuracy or shadow readiness.
