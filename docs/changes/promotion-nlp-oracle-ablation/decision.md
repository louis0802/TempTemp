# Decision: STOP_AUTONOMOUS_EXTRACTION

The requested study is implemented and executed. The results do not justify continuing general-LLM autonomous extraction architecture research on the current contracts. Keep NLP non-authoritative for candidate enrichment, reviewer assistance, missing-fact suggestions and classification support; deterministic source adapters and verified publication evidence remain authoritative. No production integration or activation is made. A2 remains unauthorized.

Deterministic Stage 1 is a viable replacement for research/reviewer evidence inventory in this cohort: it covers every reviewed literal/anchor and E meets the A-gap target. It does not make proposition ownership or normalization safe. No additional extraction/reasoning layer or semantic graph is recommended automatically.

Primary bottleneck: Stage 5 normalization/required representation completeness and provenance. It loses the largest portion of the prior 228-condition ledger (75), fails the conditional contract/completeness targets, invents a clock range and misuses physical/redemption domains. Secondary bottleneck: Stage 3 ownership, with two definite Student Meal cross-offer errors and 88 missing annotated edges even with oracle nodes/propositions. Stage 2 is also a conditional recovery bottleneck; the Student Meal merge remains while Bari can separate. Taxonomy has zero false economics and correct contests, but 83.72% economic recall and weak launch specificity. This is conditional capability/representation weakness together with compounded V4 survival, not orchestration alone.

These are conditional quality results GIVEN perfect reviewed upstream input (reviewed benchmark oracle), not objective perfect semantic truth or end-to-end recall. E instead conditions on deterministic spans. One sample per unit measures this cohort, not repeated-sample reliability. The benchmark is minimum annotation: broad-anchor ambiguity, harmless subdivisions, citation defects, scope equivalence and structured-field retention receive separate source review. Frozen scores, thresholds and raw responses remain unchanged; derived precision and identity-contract ledger clarify measurement limits.
