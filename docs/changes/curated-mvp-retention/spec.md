# Curated MVP retention specification
1. Every curated record remains represented with ready, needs_validity, needs_location or needs_content_resolution status. Offer wording need not prove savings; preserve the source proposition.
2. Independently expose content, validity and mapping state. Unsupported text or missing merchant/title/proposition requires content review. Online-only records have no pins and never trigger physical discovery.
3. Missing, unbounded or conflicting validity remains unresolved; never infer an expiry. Live physical feed requires resolved content, complete valid active dates and accepted Google outlets.
4. Development /corpus shows all records, including incomplete, online-only, expired and upcoming, independent of viewport. Cards/details label unresolved fields and lifecycle and tolerate absent outlets/dates.
5. Preserve named-versus-merchant Google resolution and source restrictions. Locations never assert participation.
6. Historical benchmark structure labels remain historical metadata, not inclusion gates. Review every changed baseline record against original text and retain a per-record audit of all 79 prior exclusions.
7. Report totals, content/validity/map dimensions, status and lifecycle plus malformed/unsupported records. All requested suites must pass; finish with a local commit and clean tree.
No open product decisions. Ready online content means content/validity ready with mapStatus online_only; it never qualifies for the physical live feed.
