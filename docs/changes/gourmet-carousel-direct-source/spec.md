# Gourmet Carousel direct source — specification

## Scope and journeys

A one-shot preview acquires only registered official roots and approved visible links, reports every discovered relevant path classification, and emits only isolated campaigns explicitly associated with Gourmet Carousel. Operator ownership never assigns campaigns to a venue. Ordinary menus, buffet descriptions, prices, vouchers and service information alone do not establish a promotion.

A source with incomplete enumeration remains shadow: preview works and ingest rejects it before database access. Activation requires independent operator/domain evidence, exact venue relationship, complete listing and service-page coverage, resolved controls and classification, deterministic extraction and replay, authoritative venue participation, successful bounded live acquisition and the unchanged publication gate.

## Acceptance criteria

- AC1: Exact roots/hosts and observed cards, links, pagination, filters, AJAX and service-page campaign behavior have immutable response metadata. No recursive same-site crawl.
- AC2: All discovered paths have deterministic promotion/non-promotion/unresolved classifications. Other or ambiguous venue offers never become Gourmet Carousel campaigns. Changed structure and unknown controls fail completeness.
- AC3: Candidates cite explicit title, merchant, benefit, description and any validity, schedule, participation, eligibility, redemption and terms. Unknown facts stay null/issues; metadata/menu/widget/current dates never supply campaign validity. Multi-campaign facts cannot leak.
- AC4: Official evidence establishes Gourmet Carousel venue identity/address independently of the operator. A provider uses that evidence for physical identity; places supply coordinates only. Changed identity and ambiguous participation fail closed.
- AC5: Shared lifecycle excludes explicitly expired campaigns before outlet lookup; missing validity reviews; complete facts with proven acquisition/venue can pass the unchanged gate. Shadow remains disabled.
- AC6: Existing Pepper/Shake, Paradise disabled state, persistence/review/dedupe, processor version and Telegram isolation remain intact. Frozen evidence/source-monitor storage/next-env.d.ts remain unchanged.
- AC7: Regenerate the 139-row map from frozen inputs using current registry status only; report before/after counts without manual adjustment.

## Product decisions

No unresolved decision requires approval: incomplete coverage mandates shadow. Exact registered roots, current venue semantics, candidate facts and blockers will be finalized from captured official HTML. Full hotel campaign coverage is outside this merchant scope and will not be claimed.

## Final observed delivery

Two Gourmet campaigns are extracted: Coffee Day at the officially identified Barista counter, and Moon Pastry complimentary delivery for qualifying bulk orders. The latter retains unknown promotion dates/physical participation. Both are review-only under the actual disabled source policy. The counter and buffet restaurant are not aliases. Current source coverage is explicitly partial despite nine resolved path classifications; complete service-page discovery is an activation blocker.
