## Limitations

This is controlled local regression evidence, not production recall evidence. The tracker correctly reports zero sealed rev6 core replacement intervals in existing local data. The initial gate still requires at least three reviewed rev6 replacement-eligible days; preferred evidence is five to seven plus researcher-assessed benchmark diversity/sample sufficiency. No worker was deployed or scheduled. Supplemental review is optional and absence is reported as unknown, not zero. Historical merged candidates without retained per-source evidence are conservatively excluded from projection rather than reconstructed with unsupported facts.

Final self-review checked revision dispatch, frozen hashes/roles, core-only projection/review identities, null-versus-zero semantics, timing provenance, supplemental diagnostics, cumulative family separation and active/sealed lifecycle handling. One additional finding was fixed: supplemental-only counts remain null while core content is unscorable, rather than treating an unknown primary result as a miss. This was a self-review, not an independent review.
