# Intent

Telegram promotion links often lead to publishers or other intermediaries rather than the original offer source. Research should establish which destination and adapter patterns recur before investing in direct source adapters.

Audit the latest 50 promotion signals from sgfooddeals and tastesoulsg, retaining inspected non-offer posts separately. Success means reproducible provenance, explicit unresolved ownership, useful unweighted family/domain counts, and an actual local report.

Telegram, publishers and social posts are discovery signals. Direct merchant/issuer sources are production evidence candidates only after ownership and offer facts are verified. This work is research-only: no production ingestion, database operations, registry population, revision-6 recovery, recurring monitor, worker, commits or pushes. Existing source-substitution research remains historical context and is preserved.
